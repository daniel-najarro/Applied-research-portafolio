## Selected data projects

### Multidimensional Poverty Measurement and Dashboard Development in Peru
**Project:** Multidimensional Poverty Dashboard – ComexPerú
**Role:** Lead Researcher, Methodology Designer, Statistical Analyst, and Dashboard Developer.
**Focus:** Measurement of multidimensional poverty and socioeconomic deprivation in Peru, incorporating inequalities in access to healthcare, education, and adequate housing.
**Primary analytical tool:** Stata.
**Data source:** Peru's National Household Survey (ENAHO), including the health, education, housing and household characteristics, and summary modules.
**Methodology:** Alkire-Foster multidimensional poverty measurement framework, with an adapted weighting structure reflecting the socioeconomic characteristics and realities of Peru.

#### Project description
I independently designed and implemented a multidimensional poverty measurement framework for Peru, applying the Alkire-Foster methodology and adapting the indicator weighting structure to the country's socioeconomic context.

The project involved processing and integrating multiple ENAHO survey modules, constructing deprivation indicators, calculating the multidimensional poverty index, and developing an interactive dashboard to communicate the findings.

The analysis incorporates healthcare access and related deprivations as a specific dimension of multidimensional poverty, alongside education and housing conditions.

#### My specific contributions
I was responsible for the complete analytical and methodological development of the project, including:

- **Methodological design:** Developed the multidimensional poverty measurement framework based on the Alkire-Foster methodology, adapting indicator selection and weights to the Peruvian context.
- **Data processing and integration:** Cleaned, transformed, and integrated information from multiple ENAHO survey modules using Stata.
- **Indicator construction:** Designed and calculated socioeconomic deprivation indicators across the selected dimensions.
- **Statistical estimation:** Conducted the statistical calculations required to estimate multidimensional poverty indicators.
- **Dashboard development:** Independently developed the interactive dashboard to communicate findings and facilitate the exploration of multidimensional poverty indicators.
- **Policy-oriented analysis:** Translated statistical results into evidence to support discussions on socioeconomic inequality, access to essential services, and public policy priorities.
