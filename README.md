About me
I am an economist specializing in labor markets, employment, labor informality, productivity, social protection, and evidence-based public policy. I work in Economic Studies at the Sociedad de Comercio Exterior del Perú (ComexPerú), where my research interests include regional labor-market performance, socioeconomic inequalities, and the use of quantitative evidence to inform policy discussions.
My background combines applied economic research and statistical data analysis, particularly with Stata, as well as work relating to multidimensional poverty, health-system challenges, and the development of analytical outputs and dashboards. I have basic proficiency in R and am strengthening it for data manipulation, visualization, and reproducible analytical workflows.
I am particularly interested in connecting labor-market vulnerability, multidimensional poverty, health outcomes, and climate-related risks through interdisciplinary research and data harmonization.
Areas of expertise
Labor economics and employment policy: employment conditions, formal and informal work, labor-market competitiveness, wages, and productivity.
Social protection and public policy: unemployment insurance, social-security coverage, and institutional and policy challenges.
Socioeconomic inequality: multidimensional poverty, access to essential services, and regional disparities.
Applied quantitative analysis: statistical data processing, construction and interpretation of indicators, and research reporting.
Health-related data and policy: health-system performance, access to healthcare, and processing data for monitoring dashboards.
